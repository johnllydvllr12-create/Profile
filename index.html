Profile & Perspective

Background & Focus

How academic coursework, practical projects, and personal discipline shape my engineering mindset.

My engineering education began with a core curiosity about how computers function at the lowest level — from logic gates and microcontrollers up to networked software.

Throughout my degree at ICCT Colleges, I have focused on translating theoretical engineering concepts into tangible implementations. That work has included drafting 2D facility layouts and structured cabling blueprints in AutoCAD, calculating network bandwidth and routing topologies for commercial buildings, and configuring ESP32 microcontrollers for low-latency wireless hardware triggering.

In software development, I build on strong foundations in C++, emphasizing clean object-oriented architecture, data structure efficiency, and memory management. On the web side, I develop accessible interfaces using HTML5 and CSS3, prioritizing responsive layouts and cross-browser consistency without depending on bloated third-party frameworks.

I take pride in reliability and disciplined execution. Beyond the screen, consistent strength training at the gym reinforces the patience and mental grit required to methodically isolate and solve difficult technical problems.
